Add it in Gmail

  1. In Gmail, open Settings (the gear), then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and choose Create new. Call it TAV.
  3. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose TAV for new emails and for replies and forwards.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and press Save changes. Then send yourself a test email.

Add it in Outlook

  1. Open Settings, then Mail, then Compose and reply.
  2. Paste into the signature box, set it as the default, and save.
